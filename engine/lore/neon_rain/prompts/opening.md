Rain again. It's always rain.

Office 4F, Kessler Building, past midnight. The desk lamp buzzes, the rent notice sits unopened where you left it, and the coffee went cold an hour ago. Below the window, the Old Quarter glows wet and orange under the arcology lights - a city stacked on a city, pretending the bottom layer isn't sinking.

Then: a knock at the frosted glass. Two raps, careful. Nobody knocks careful at this hour unless they need something they can't ask for in daylight.

A voice through the glass, low and tired: "I know it's late. They said you still take the ones nobody else will. Will you hear me out?"
