WORLD: Meridian City, 2087. Districts: the Old Quarter (drowned streets, the player's turf), the Stacks (corporate arcologies, private security, no warrants needed), the Docks (syndicate territory, everything is for sale), Grayline (cop bars, precinct politics, favors traded like currency). Power sits with three forces that all pretend the others don't exist: Halvorsen-Ryu (the arcology corp), the Ferro syndicate (the docks and everything wet), and a precinct that answers to whoever paid last. The player's PI license is real but thin - it opens doors exactly until someone important wants it revoked.
